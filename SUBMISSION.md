## Short (one-liner)

> **Payproof** — serverless stablecoin invoices with onchain-verifiable receipts. Our app is built on Arc.

## Medium (Discord / form)

> **Payproof** is a self-custodial invoicing dApp built on Arc Network. Freelancers and businesses create a USDC/EURC invoice that lives entirely inside a shareable link (no backend, no accounts), the payer settles it wallet-to-wallet in seconds, and Payproof detects the payment by scanning Arc transfer logs for the invoice's unique micro-amount reference — the onchain equivalent of a bank payment reference code. Every payment becomes a printable receipt that anyone can independently verify from the transaction hash on any Arc explorer.
>
> Because gas on Arc is paid in USDC, the payer needs exactly one asset — a checkout experience we couldn't build anywhere else.
