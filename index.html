How it works

  • 🔗
    Serverless by design The whole invoice travels inside the link — no database, no account, nothing to shut down.
  • 🧾
    Payment reference, onchain Each invoice gets a unique micro-amount signature (like a bank reference code). Payproof scans Arc transfer logs and matches the payment automatically.
  • ✅
    Verifiable receipts Every receipt maps to a transaction hash on Arc — your client, your accountant, anyone can verify it independently.
  • ⛽
    One asset, everything On Arc, gas is paid in USDC — your payer needs exactly one token. No juggling a second coin for fees.
